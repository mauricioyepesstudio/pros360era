# EVOLUSA — MVP

## Outcome

Prove that stage-based guidance creates more qualified, trustworthy conversations than a generic service catalog. This is a marketing and qualification product—not the full portal.

## Scope

### Phase 1 — Marketing foundation

- Repositioned Home and message hierarchy.
- Six-option Stage Selector and Journey.
- Stage-aware services with provider disclosures.
- Roadmap preview, Cómo funciona, trust, compliant FAQ, final CTA.
- Responsive header/footer and foundational legal pages.
- Verified contact channels only.

### Phase 2 — Roadmap questionnaire

- 4–8 adaptive questions.
- Deterministic, testable recommendation rules.
- Completado / Ahora / Próximamente results.
- Contextual disclaimers and safe escalation.
- Anonymous use; optional consent-based delivery/lead capture.

### Phase 3 — Conversion operations

- Lead capture with consent and response expectations.
- WhatsApp deep link with non-sensitive stage/goal context.
- Booking through an approved provider.
- CRM adapter and field mapping.
- Consent-aware analytics.

### Not in MVP

Accounts, persistent portal, documents, payments/subscriptions, AI assistant, marketplace transactions, complex tracking/notifications, or full bilingual operations.

## User stories

- A newcomer can identify a stage without service terminology.
- An entrepreneur sees a sequence, not a flat catalog.
- An owner distinguishes protection/operations from customer growth.
- A cautious visitor sees who provides each offering.
- A user gets an initial roadmap without an account.
- A lead can continue by form, WhatsApp, or booking and knows what happens next.
- An operator receives stage/goal context without unnecessary sensitive data.

## Questionnaire boundaries

Ask: current situation, next goal, whether a business exists, self-reported foundations, biggest obstacle, state (with purpose), and preferred continuation. Avoid sensitive identifiers, immigration details, revenue/debt, or documents before a defined secure need exists.

## Minimum lead contract

- name;
- preferred contact method;
- phone or email as appropriate;
- language preference and state;
- selected stage and primary goal;
- roadmap/rules version;
- separate marketing and service-contact consent;
- source and timestamp.

Never place sensitive answers in WhatsApp URLs, analytics, or public result URLs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Home consistently supports “next step”.
- Every offer has provider type and reviewed claims.
- Roadmap works on mobile and keyboard.
- Outputs are deterministic, explainable, and tested.
- All CTAs work and are semantically valid.
- Server validation, failure handling, spam control, consent.
- No unsupported metrics, testimonials, or professional claims.
- Privacy, terms, disclosures, accessibility are linked.
- Responsive, metadata, headings, focus, contrast, performance QA.
- Analytics carries no unnecessary personal/sensitive data.
- Spanish editorial and compliance review completed.

## Measurement

Activation: Stage Selector completion followed by Roadmap start. Conversion: qualified continuation via form, WhatsApp, or booking. Also measure completion/abandonment, result actions, stage/channel conversion, spam, handoff outcome, accessibility, and performance. Establish internal targets only after baseline data and operational capacity are known.

## Launch dependencies

- Verify legal entity and public brand name.
- Verify ownership of domain, email, phone, WhatsApp.
- Inventory direct services and fulfillment owner.
- Verify professional/partner credentials, jurisdictions, referral terms/disclosures.
- Obtain image/testimonial consent and substantiate claims.
- Approve retention, deletion, privacy, consent, incident process.
- Define lead owner, hours, response expectation, escalation.
- Select CRM/booking/analytics after requirements and privacy review.

## Delivery increments

1. Claims matrix, typed domain data, design tokens, semantic primitives.
2. Home shell and all strategic sections.
3. Roadmap schemas/rules/questionnaire/results/tests.
4. Lead endpoint, WhatsApp, booking, CRM, consent.
5. Analytics, accessibility, performance, and launch review.

No increment requires payments, domain purchase, campaigns, push, or production publishing.

## Implementation reference

Phase 2 supplies typed data, a deterministic Roadmap v1, reusable primitives, and unintegrated Home sections. CRM, analytics, booking, payments, accounts, and portal remain unimplemented as required.
