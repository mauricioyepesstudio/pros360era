# EvolUSA — Founder validation brief
Date: 2026-11-12
Status: business hypothesis; not a verified market opportunity

## What it is
EvolUSA is a multi-profession digital marketplace and growth operating system. Each provider can have a profession-specific sales page, service catalog, gallery, intake, ARM/CRM, reporting and approved automations/agents. Customers can discover a provider, describe a need and request contact. Some live matching categories exist; the full catalog and automation features remain in progress.

## For whom
Initial B2B customer: independent bilingual professionals and small service businesses operating in Florida, with a preference to validate in South Florida, including document-preparation services, notaries, financial/operations advisors, marketers and contractors. Realtor, tax and insurance categories are product demos only until respective data models and verification gates are developed. Secondary consumer: people and companies looking for a service provider.

## Product / offer
- Provider: public commercial profile with category-specific presentation and lead-follow-up workspace.
- Optional advanced offerings (not yet available across categories): marketing management, social content sync, lead qualification, dashboards, automations, booking and sales follow-up.
- Existing accounts: pilot participants should not be charged during the previously agreed pilot.
- Future pricing: NOT finalized. Avoid announcing a subscription fee as live until offer and costs are verified.
- Any paid external usage such as WhatsApp, advertising, AI API, domains, email/communications, and third-party subscriptions is scoped/quoted separately.

## Where / how
Web: https://evolusa.vercel.app
Source: mauricioyepesstudio/pros360era; Next.js, Supabase, Vercel.
Public working directory: /profesionales; isolated fictional demos: /demo/profesionales.
Proposed acquisition: referrals from existing professionals and demonstrated results, outreach to local small businesses, website inquiries, and controlled agency sales.
Delivery: online SaaS plus scoped onboarding/service assistance; professional-specific modules enabled only after compliance and technical verification.

## Constraints and known gaps
- Live profiles exist; fictional templates must stay outside the real public directory and matching.
- Currently supported opportunity intake is authenticated and limited by live database categories.
- Public professional-specific lead capture, tenant-scoped ARM, galleries, social sync and autonomous agents are incomplete; avoid claiming they function.
- Professional categories needing licenses require appropriate verification and jurisdiction checks.
- Do not invent testimonials, conversions, market size, prices, approval status, or agent outputs.
- No payment to incumbent pilot profiles; Stripe integration must be separately verified before testing paid onboarding.
- Confidential customer data must not be used in demo personas.

## Open data required before financial verdict
Final monthly plans and offer limits; hosting/integration cost by active tenant; customer support hours and cost; verified outreach conversion rate; lead conversion to paying professionals; cancellation/retention; ad/customer acquisition costs; staff costs; sales tax/compliance applicability where relevant; funnel instrumentation.
